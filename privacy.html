Privacy policy

For the Google Drive option in Kryoto Desktop. Last updated 18 November 2026.

What Kryoto Desktop accesses

When you connect Google Drive, Kryoto Desktop asks Google for one permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. With it, the app can see and change only the files and folders it created itself. It cannot see, read or change anything else in your Drive.

It also reads your Google account's email address, to show you which account is connected.

What it stores, and where

  • In your Google Drive, in a folder named Kryoto Saves: backups of your game saves (.ksave files), a list of those backups (index.json) and your unlocked achievements (achievements.json).
  • On your PC: your Google account's email address, in Kryoto Desktop's settings, and the sign-in Google gives the app, in your system's secure credential store (Windows Credential Manager, or GNOME Keyring / KWallet on Linux).

What is shared

Nothing. Your data goes only between your PC and your own Google Drive. It is not sent to the developer, to kryo.to or to anyone else, and it is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI or machine learning models.

Kryoto Desktop's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • Press Disconnect in Kryoto Desktop's Settings → Cloud. This revokes the app's access and deletes the sign-in from your PC.
  • You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the Kryoto Saves folder in Google Drive to remove the backups themselves.
