Kryoto Desktop: Google Drive saves

Kryoto Desktop is a game launcher for Windows and Linux. Its Google Drive option keeps backups of your game saves and your unlocked achievements in your own Google Drive, so every PC you connect with the same Google account can get them back.

How it works

  • In Kryoto Desktop, open Settings → Cloud and choose Google Drive under “Where your saves are kept” or “Where your achievements are kept”.
  • Your browser opens Google's sign-in page. Once you allow access, Kryoto makes a folder called Kryoto Saves in your Drive.
  • When a game closes, its saves are backed up there. Before a game starts, newer saves from another PC are put back. Older versions are kept so you can go back to them.
  • Kryoto asks only for the drive.file permission: it can see and change the files it made itself, and nothing else in your Drive.

Stopping

Press Disconnect in Settings → Cloud, or remove Kryoto Desktop's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Your Kryoto Saves folder stays in your Drive until you delete it.
